Practice

There are three kinds of practice. Exercises are done together in class. Vignettes are done together in recitation. Homework is done with your classmates or on your own outside of class. Practice is built into the course in this way to give you regular and repeated practice before a checkpoint. Practice is graded for completion, carries no points for your grade, and is required to unlock the checkpoint.

Practice can only be made up after the checkpoint.

  1. Find the Exercise, Vignette, or Homework you missed on the course page and complete the work.
  2. Bring the completed work to Taylor’s or your TA’s office hours.
  3. Reattempt the skills you still need.

Skillsheet Reports

You'll receive a skillsheet report showing the practice you've completed on the path to attempting the skill and the skills you've passed. Your final grade is simply the percentage of skills you pass.
