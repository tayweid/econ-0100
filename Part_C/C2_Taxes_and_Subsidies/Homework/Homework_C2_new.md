## Q1 | The Butterbeer Tax

$$
D: P_B = 40 - Q_D \qquad\qquad S: P_S = 10 + \frac{1}{2} Q_S
$$

Use a graph and algebra to illustrate what happened to the market before and after the tax. Start with the pre-tax equilibrium. Then use the tax equation ($P_B = \tau + P_S$) to solve for equilibrium quantity and the two prices. Conclude with the remaining relevant components.
