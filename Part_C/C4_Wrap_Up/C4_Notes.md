### 1 | One rule for deadweight loss

> **1.a.** Title “Finding Deadweight Loss”; subtitle “Four markets, one rule.” The plain market: MB, MC, red Q̂ = 40. Bottom: “Where is the deadweight loss?”

> **1.b.** The “Finding DWL” card reveals its five steps one at a time, right of the graph: 1. Society’s curves. 2. Q\*: where society’s curves cross. 3. Q: what the market trades. 4. One unit: social benefit vs. social cost. 5. Every unit between Q and Q\*. Bottom: “The same five steps find it in every market in Part C.”

### 2 | Negative externality: all three stages

> **2.a.** Title “Negative Externalities.” Step 1: pink unit bars over MC bring in MSC, then fade. Bottom: “Society counts the external cost: MSC = MC + EXT.”

> **2.a.quantities.** Steps 2 and 3: yellow Q\* = 32, then the red Q̂ dot pulses. Bottom: “Q\* = 32, where MB meets MSC. The market ignores EXT and trades Q̂ = 40.”

> **2.b.select.** Step 4. The 36th unit as a faint bar (MB, MC, EXT) in a yellow selection box. Bottom: “Take one unit between Q\* and Q̂: the 36th.”

> **2.b.values → 2.b.private.compare → 2.b.private → 2.b.social.compare → 2.b.social.** Stage 1, C1’s close-up of the 36th unit (subtitle “The 36th unit: between Q\* and Q̂.”) with the market and card faded out: MB \$4.80, MC \$3.80, pink EXT up to MSC \$5.80; Buyer and Seller; both questions. The market compares MB with MC (check); society compares MB with MSC (cross, DWL), with grey DWL on the seller’s stack from \$4.80 to \$5.80. Bottom: “Trading the 36th unit costs society \$1: its deadweight loss.”

> **2.c.** Stage 2. The close-up compresses onto the market around the 36th unit’s line, every piece landing on its counterpart: the columns on the small floating bars either side of the line, the edges on the flanged edges, values and names on theirs, the brackets on the brackets, the check and cross on their places beside them, the grey loss on the gap on the seller bar. > **2.d.inside.quantity → .mb → .mc → .msc → .values → 2.d.inside.** The 28th unit: the picture clears to the line and the dots, which slide along the curves to 28 with the ordinal counting. > **2.d.outside.quantity → .mb → .mc → .msc → .values → 2.d.outside.** Repeat the paused quantity → MB → MC → MSC reveal at the 44th unit, then drop the bars.
