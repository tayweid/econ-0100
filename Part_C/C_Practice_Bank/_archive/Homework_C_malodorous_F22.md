#### Question 1 | Malodorous Byproducts

Butterbeer is beloved by Hogwarts students and teachers alike and is widely available at wizarding shops. But the beverage has the negative effect of generating displeasing odors from its imbibers. Model the private market for butterbeer using private and social cost and benefit curves. Indicate
on your graph the private equilibrium quantity and social equilibrium quantity. Using your economic policy advising credentials, offer a remedy to the malodorous byproduct the Ministry could implement. And conclude with a description of the effects your policy would have on the butterbeer market.
