## Nuances

- The notes are prep material before class, not a script.
- There was too much. At least one part of an exercise should come up front.
- Core scenes instead of one long animation.
- We don’t need the growing of the market. Teach bidding, switching, the single price, and deliberation on small stages, then show them for one or two players in the large one.
- Stay head on for multiple trades. One buyer highlighted, two options in two boxes.
- One person per bar. If someone wants more than one, they show up twice.
- The price window only appears in the first exchange.
- Supply and demand separate to start. Stack them vertically to show the quantities are equal.
- Spend plenty of time on equilibrium on the graph.
- Excess, not surplus.
- It should set up taxes, international trade, and externalities.
- We spent too long on the specifics of the market dynamics.

 

# B4 Outline

Do welfare at the market level. 

1. Show one exchange, setting the price very low with a price control, thinking through quantity exchanged, and so on.
2. Then thinking about incentives in the price control, and then a move to equilibrium, and how welfare changed.
3. Then more quickly show a high price, doing it with TS maybe here.
4. Then force a few more people to exchange and see what happens to TS.
5. Then do first welfare theorem.

 

## Sep 23 | Used in Class

1. Review B3 in seven stages, with Exercise B3 Q2 before equilibrium.
2. The gains from trade. CS and PS on one exchange become total surplus.
3. A price floor at \$6. The lost gains, then one pair that can’t trade.
4. A price ceiling at \$3. The lost gains, then one pair that can’t trade. Exercise B4 Q1.
5. Give that pair the trade. The market without controls allows all beneficial trades.
6. Trades beyond equilibrium. Negative TS.
7. First welfare theorem. Exercise B4 Q2.

## Sep 22 | Proposed, Back Half

1. How much does the market benefit buyers and sellers. Total surplus.
2. Can we increase it by raising the price? DWL is the TS we could have had. Zoom into a pair that doesn’t trade because of the price control.
3. Then lower the price. Show the DWL again with a specific pair.
4. Give them the trade. TS is maximized by allowing all these people to trade.
5. We don’t want anything more. Force the next pair to trade and they lose benefit. Negative TS.
