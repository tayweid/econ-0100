# Tutorial | Duopoly and Oligopoly

## Question 1

Suppose the demand for wands is given by $Q = 100 - P$ and there are two wands sellers, A and B that produce at a constant marginal cost of 10. The marginal revenue for Acme is $MR_a = 100 - 2q_A - q_B$ and for Beta it is $MR_b = 100 - 2q_B - q_A$. Calculate the Nash equilibrium level of output for the two firms.

## Question 2

Suppose a third wandmaker, C, enters the market. The demand for wands remains $Q = 100 - P$, and all three wandmakers produce at a constant marginal cost of 10. The marginal revenue for A is $MR_a = 100 - 2q_A - q_B - q_C$ and for B it is $MR_b = 100 - 2q_B - q_A - q_B$ and for C it is $MR_b = 100 - 2q_B - q_A - q_B$. Calculate the Nash equilibrium level of output for the three firms.
