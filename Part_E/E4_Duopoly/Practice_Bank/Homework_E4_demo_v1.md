# Question 3 | Parallel Wandmaker's Dillemma 

Suppose the demand for wands is given by 

$$P = 100 - Q$$

and there are two main wand sellers, Olivander and Gregorovitch. Olivander can make wands at a constant marginal cost of $10$, while Gregorovitch can make wands at a constant marginal cost of $5$. The marginal revenue for Olivander is 

$$MR_O = 100 - 2q_O - q_G$$ 

and for Gregorovitch it is

$$MR_G = 100 - 2q_G - q_O$$ 
 
Units are in galleons and stones. Calculate the Nash equilibrium level of output for the two wandsellers and put it in the blanks below.
