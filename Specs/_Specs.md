# ECON 0100 | Skills by Block

Canonical skill list. **Blocks** are class sessions — one video, one notes doc, one in-class exercise handout each. **Skills** are the individually assessed items inside them (pass/fail per skill on MEs). Code scheme: block `A1`, skill `A1.1`. Old flat codes → `CODES.md`.

Evidence tags: **\[ME\]** tested on a 24F MiniExam · **\[P\]** practiced in 24F but never on an ME · **\[new\]** no material exists. Tier: **Core** (20) / **Ext** (13) — see grade table below.

## Part A | The Landscape

- **A0 intro** — part opener, not assessed
- **A1 Tradeoffs**

    - A1.1 *Opportunity Cost* (Core) — compute the opportunity cost of a choice from a scenario or production table. \[ME\]
  - A1.2 *Marginal Reasoning* (Ext) — evaluate a decision by marginal cost/benefit; identify a Pareto improvement. \[P\]
- **A2 The PPF**

    - A2.1 *The PPF* (Core) — construct a PPF; classify bundles; shift it for labor or productivity changes. \[ME\]
- **A3 Advantage**

    - A3.1 *Absolute & Comparative Advantage* (Core) — build the opportunity-cost table; identify who has each advantage. \[ME\]
- **A4 Trade**

    - A4.1 *Specialization & Trade* (Core) — assign specialization, feasible exchange rates, post-trade consumption. \[ME\]

## Part B | Markets

- **B0 intro**
- **B1 Demand & Supply**

    - B1.1 *Demand* (Core) — plot/read demand, Qd, CS, shifters. \[ME\]
  - B1.2 *Supply* (Core) — plot/read supply, Qs, PS, shifters. \[ME\]
- **B2 Equilibrium & Welfare**

    - B2.1 *Equilibrium* (Core) — solve P\* and Q\*; shortage/surplus incentives. \[ME\]
  - B2.2 *Market Welfare* (Core) — total CS and PS at equilibrium; why the competitive quantity maximizes surplus. \[ME\]
- **B3 Market Changes**

    - B3.1 *Comparative Statics* (Core) — which curves shift, direction of P and Q effects. \[ME\]
  - B3.2 *Elasticity* (Ext) — midpoint elasticity; cross-price and income interpretation. \[P\]
- **B4 Price Controls**

    - B4.1 *Price Controls* (Ext) — ceiling/floor: prices, quantities, welfare, DWL. \[ME\]

## Part C | Market Failure

- **C0 intro**
- **C1 Taxes & Subsidies**

    - C1.1 *Taxes & Incidence* (Core) — buyer/seller prices, quantity, revenue, DWL, burden. \[ME\]
  - C1.2 *Subsidies* (Ext) — same mechanics for a subsidy. \[P\]
- **C2 Trade & Tariffs**

    - C2.1 *International Trade & Tariffs* (Ext) — world price: imports/exports, surplus transfers; tariff effects. \[ME\]
- **C3 Externalities**

    - C3.1 *Negative Externalities* (Core) — market vs socially efficient Q; DWL area. \[ME\]
  - C3.2 *Positive Externalities* (Core) — same with social MB. \[ME\]
- **C4 Corrective Policy**

    - C4.1 *Corrective Policy* (Ext) — instrument choice and size to eliminate DWL; marginal-unit intuition. \[ME\]

## Part D | Strategic Interaction

- **D0 intro**
- **D1 Games**

    - D1.1 *Best Response* (Core) — 2×2 payoff matrix: best responses, dominant strategies. \[ME\]
  - D1.2 *Nash Equilibrium* (Core) — all Nash equilibria vs the socially efficient outcome; name the dilemma. \[ME\]
- **D2 The Commons**

    - D2.1 *Goods & the Commons* (Ext) — rivalry × excludability; commons as a game. \[P\]
- **D3 Public Goods & Voting**

    - D3.1 *Public Goods* (Core) — WTP aggregation, MSB vs cost, efficient provision, free-riding. \[ME\]
  - D3.2 *Voting* (Ext) — majority outcome vs efficiency; cycles. \[ME\]
- **D4 Sequential Games**

    - D4.1 *Sequential Games* (Ext) — game trees, backward induction. \[new\]

## Part E | Sellers

- **E0 intro**
- **E1 Costs** — E1.1 *Costs of Production* (Core) — production function → TC/VC/FC/ATC/AVC/MC. \[P\]
- **E2 Competition** — E2.1 *Competitive Firms* (Core) — P = MC, entry/exit, long-run zero profit, DWL = 0. \[ME\]
- **E3 Monopoly** — E3.1 *Monopoly* (Core) — MR = MC, price, DWL. \[ME\]
- **E4 Duopoly** — E4.1 *Duopoly* (Ext) — Cournot best responses vs competitive benchmark. \[ME\]
- **E5 Policy & Market Power** — E5.1 (Ext) — tax/subsidy across market structures. \[P\]

## Part F | Buyers

- **F0 intro**
- **F1 The Consumer's Problem**

    - F1.1 *Budget Constraints* (Core) — draw; shift for income; pivot for prices. \[P\]
  - F1.2 *Preferences & Optimization* (Core) — indifference curves, MRS, optimal bundle. \[P\]
- **F2 Factor Markets** — F2.1 (Ext) — labor supply/demand; wages to income. \[ME\]
- **F3 Linked Markets** — F3.1 (Ext) — trace a shock through one market to the consumer's bundle (capstone). \[ME\]

Design notes:

- Floors, not averages; all conditions must hold; Extension never substitutes for Core.
- "Passed" = pass once on a part ME (reattempts replace in your favor); Core is additionally recertified on ME_Z — an A certifies retention. Attempt count never affects the grade.
- A requires 10/13 Extensions (not all 13) while three Extension banks (C1.2, D4.1, E5.1) are first-semester new; tighten later.
- Reattempt policy may differ by tier (e.g. unlimited Core reattempts).
- Pair with a checkbox tracker mirroring the table rows.
- New ME stems needed: A1.2, B3.2, C1.2, D2.1, D4.1, E1.1, E5.1 (+ standalone F1.1/F1.2 and C1.1-incidence stems). Core ones first: E1.1, F1.1, F1.2.
