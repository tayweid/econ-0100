## ECON 0100 | Taylor’s Series Videos

This video series is an attempt to tell a cohesive story of Microeconomics using visualizations and animations. economics from scratch.

### Style Guide

- for the videos, there's the direct to camera, the animation, b roll, but also a sort of note taking part that keeps track of what's been covered and adds to it like someone following along taking notes, maybe an iPad or like Phill Edwards using Excel
