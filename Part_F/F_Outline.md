## Part F | Buyers and Preferences

*Buyers decisions make up the demand curve.*

This part aims to develop the model behind the demand curve.

We’re going to start by thinking about how the individual chooses quantity. This is demand, which is the result of the budget constraint and preferences represented by the indifference space. And given some quantity, which buyers have no direct control over, prices happen according to Edgeworth. It makes everyone happy given their endowment.

Construct the individual demand curve. Then the demand curve.
