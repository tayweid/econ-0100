ECON 0100 · A guide to your progress

Checkpoints & reattempts

Practice unlocks a skill. A checkpoint is your first chance to pass it. Reattempts give you more chances.

Passed skills stay passed. You only redo skills you haven’t passed yet.

    Practice is checked for completion, not correctness. These requirements apply to each skill, not to your total assignments for the part.

    03

    Missed practice? Here’s the path back.

    After the checkpoint, you can make up missed practice for a reattempt.

    1. Complete it.

      Finish the Exercise, Vignette, or Homework you missed.

    2. Bring it to us.

      Bring the completed work to Taylor or your TA to have the practice recorded.

    3. Try the skill.

      With 3 of 3 complete, try the skill at a reattempt offering that part.

    Makeups are for after the checkpoint. You cannot make up practice to unlock the original checkpoint. Still attend it and answer the skills you have unlocked.
