# Homework 0 — Fall 2021 | PPF Practice

## Question

After the war, as residents of Hogsmeade entered the food service industry, some witches and wizards remained in magical occupations. Food service in Hogsmeade was split between brewing butterbeer, where one worker could produce 10 pints per day, and baking cornish pasties, where kitchens became congested with too many bakers. One such witch developed a method for brewing butterbeer much more quickly. This enabled anyone familiar with the process to brew twice as much as they were previously able to. Use a four quadrant PPF to explain what happened to the food service after the new brewing method was widely adopted.
