# Part A Practice Bank

Part A practice material from past semesters that isn't already covered by Fall 2026's practice. This folder replaced the per-block `Practice_Bank` folders in A1–A3 on 2026-09-28. The 2021 and 2022 demos had been cut into PPF, advantage, and trade pieces across the three blocks, and the same solution PDFs had been copied into all three, so most files existed two to four times. Each file keeps its PDF and ink solutions beside it. The suffix is the source semester; files without one have no clear source.

"Covers" lists the Fall 2026 blocks: A1 opportunity cost and the PPF, A2 absolute and comparative advantage, A3 specialization and trade.
