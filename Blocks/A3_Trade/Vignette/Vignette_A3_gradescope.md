## Question 1 — title: `Trade` · 1 pt

```
Colin Creevey can bake $$20$$ cornish pasties ($$P$$) or $$5$$ cauldron cakes ($$C$$) in one day, and Katie Bell, back on her original oven, can bake $$15$$ pasties or $$8$$ cakes.

Suppose Colin and Katie realize they can specialize and trade goods. After they specialize, which of these trades would make them both better off? Select all that apply.
[ ] $$1$$C for $$1$$P
[x] $$1$$C for $$2$$P
[x] $$1$$C for $$3$$P
[ ] $$1$$C for $$5$$P
```

## Question 2 — title: `Accept or Reject` · 1 pt

```
Katie offers Colin $$1$$ cake for $$5$$ pasties. Does Colin accept? Why or why not?
( ) Accept: $$5$$ pasties is more than Katie's cost of a cake
(x) Reject: Colin can bake a cake himself for $$4$$ pasties
( ) Accept: Colin has the comparative advantage in pasties
( ) Reject: Katie should be paying Colin
```

## Question 3 — title: `Changing Labor` · 2 pts

```

What are Colin's new daily maximums, and what is his new opportunity cost of cake?
( ) $$32$$P or $$8$$C; $$32/5$$ pasties
(x) $$32$$P or $$8$$C; $$4$$ pasties
( ) $$20$$P or $$8$$C; $$5/2$$ pasties
( ) $$32$$P or $$5$$C; $$32/5$$ pasties

How would this increase in hours impact the trade with Katie you found in Q1? Select all that apply.
[x] Colin's PPF shifts out without changing its slope.
[ ] Colin's opportunity cost of a cake rises.
[x] The range of exchange rates both would accept is unchanged.
[x] Colin has more pasties to trade away, so the trade can grow.
```
