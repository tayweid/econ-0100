#### Consumer Surplus



Demand as a relationship between my willingness and ability to pay and my quantity demanded is pernicious and complex in some important ways. For example, what if I don't have money? I'm willing but unable to pay. The demand of a rich person will be higher than the demand of a poor person, even if their subjective experience of a chocolate bar is in some sense the same. Keep these limitations in mind as we consider this model.
