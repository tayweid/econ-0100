## Tutorials

- External Video | Primer. Simulating a Market

In this tutorial sequence (labeled as 3.1 to 3.6) we start by introducing the concepts of surplus value. Its funny the standard economics terminology uses surplus both to talk about an "excess" (quantity supplied greater than quantity demanded) and the idea of consumer and producer surplus. We're going to use this confusing convention and be clear with which we mean. These topics pair with Chapter 7 in the textbook.

Last time we introduced government policy. Here we're building up a model to evaluate how good a market is to buyers and sellers. This gives us some ability to consider the effects government policy has on those it affects.

- Tutorial 3.1 Consumer Surplus
In this tutorial we introduce the concept of consumer surplus by looking at the demand curve and the difference between what a buyer pays and would be willing to pay, and adding up this difference for everyone in the market.

- Tutorial 3.2 Producer Surplus
In this tutorial we develop a model for sellers similar to consumer surplus for buyers.

- Tutorial 3.3 Efficiency
This tutorial introduces the notion that we have some way of evaluating a "best-case" that we can compare to. This best case is what we call "efficiency".

- Tutorial 3.5 Taxes and Welfare
In this tutorial we take what we know about taxes and evaluate their impact on the welfare in the market.

- Tutorial 3.4 Price Controls
And in this tutorial we evaluate the impact price controls have on welfare.

- Tutorial 3.6 Tariffs
And lastly, we bring together many ideas about government policy and welfare to examine the effects of tariffs (a tax on imports) on the market.
