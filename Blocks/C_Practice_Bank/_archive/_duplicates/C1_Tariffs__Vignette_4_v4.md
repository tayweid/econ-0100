One classically trained magical economist proposed opening the closed domestic butterbeer market to the less expensive global market while imposing an import tariff. Ministry officials were pleased with the option but also raised concerns that domestic butterbeer sellers would be harmed by the lower price.

Use a graph to illustrate what would happen to the makret after a tariff was imposed. Is there a tariff price that would alleviate the Ministry's concern about domestic sellers?
