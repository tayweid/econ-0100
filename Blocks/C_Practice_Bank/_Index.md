# Part C Practice Bank

One copy of every Part C practice handout from past semesters, merged from the C1–C4 per-block `Practice_Bank` folders on 2026-09-28. The old homework was written per Part and chopped into block pieces (the 24F Homework C existed in four blocks' banks), so 56 duplicates were set aside: 8 byte-identical copies deleted, and the reissues, contained pieces, and render-identical PDFs moved to `_archive/_duplicates/`. Every kept file has its PDF and ink solutions beside it. The suffix is the source semester. Two pure-trade handouts (the broomstick Classwork C1 and the lemon-tarts Vignette B4) were used for Part B's B6 and moved to `../B_Practice_Bank/_archive/`.

Blocks: C2 Taxes and Subsidies, C1 Externalities, C3 Corrective Policy. The old C1 Tariffs moved to Part B on 2026-10-06: trade and tariffs are one block, `Blocks/B6_International_Trade`, and old C1's built sets are in its `From_C1_Tariffs/` folder. The old C1 column below is kept as a record.
