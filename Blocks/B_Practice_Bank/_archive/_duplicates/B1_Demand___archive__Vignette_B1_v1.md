## ECON 0100 |Fall 2024 

### Vignette B1

Members of the wizarding have preferences for pumpkin pasties according to the following demand curve:
$$
P_b = 17 - \frac{1}{6} Q_d
$$
Prices are in galleons and quantity is in thousands of pasties.

#### Q1. Plot the demand curve.

Start by finding the vertical intercept: plug in a value of 0 for quantity.

Then find the horizontal intercept: plug in a value of 0 for price.
