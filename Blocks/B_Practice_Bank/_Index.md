## Exercises_Broomstick/

Exercise B1–B6 rewritten around enchanted broomsticks (Classwork C1 24F's story and curves, 16 − Q/4 and 4 + Q/4, equilibrium 24 at 10), so Exercises get a story of their own, separate from the pasty Vignettes and the butterbeer Homework. Fall 2026 keeps its pasty Exercises through B6. B1–B5 carry over this fall's Exercises with new nouns and numbers; B6 is Classwork C1 verbatim. New wording is in {++ ++}. Each file's editor note has the answers; solutions and Gradescope sheets aren't written yet.

## Vignettes_Pandemic/

Vignette B3–B5 around the pandemic food market, with new clean numbers (S: 2 + Q/2, D: 20 − Q; equilibrium 12 servings at 8 dollars). B3 is the initial equilibrium plus an empty-shelves shortage; B4 is the supply-chain shock and the ban on price gouging (a ceiling at the pre-pandemic price); B5 is elasticity, the pandemic's two impacts, and both shocks ending in "What phenomenon is this?" (inflation). Every piece of the 22F/23F/24F framing is used. No B1, B2, or B6: the story doesn't reach them. New wording is in {++ ++}; answers are in each file's editor note.
