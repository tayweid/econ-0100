## ECON 0100 | Vignette B8

#### Q1 | 

Preferences for butter beer can be represented by the following demand curve:
$$
D: P_b = 100 − \frac{1}{2}Q_b
$$
Use a graph to plot this demand curve, including the quantity demanded at both 40 galleons and 50 galleons. Then find and label the consumer surplus at these prices.

#### Q2 | 

The supply curve for butter beer can be represented by the following equation:
$$
S: P_s = 10 + \frac{2}{3}Q_s
$$
Use a graph to plot this supply curve, and find and label the producer surplus at both 40 galleons and 50 galleons.

#### Q3 | 

What is the market equilibrium price, quantity, and total surplus? Use a graph and algebra to analyze this market.

Butter beer has culturally been associated with a good time. This all changed when a high profile wizard went viral slamming the taste. At the same time an ingredient of the drink, butter extract, became incredibly diﬀicult for butter beer makers to acquire. Use a graph to discuss the effect these two events had on the butter beer market.
