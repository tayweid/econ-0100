## Question 1 — title: `Quantity Demanded` · 2 pts

```
Pumpkin pasties sell along the demand curve $$P = 12 - Q/2$$, in galleons and pasties.

a) What is the quantity demanded at 10 galleons?

( ) $$2$$ pasties
(x) $$4$$ pasties
( ) $$7$$ pasties
( ) $$10$$ pasties

b) What is the marginal benefit at a quantity of 4?

( ) $$4$$ galleons per pasty
( ) $$8$$ galleons per pasty
(x) $$10$$ galleons per pasty
( ) $$12$$ galleons per pasty
```
