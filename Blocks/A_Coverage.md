## Gaps (Fall 2026)

Every skill has at least one Exercise and one Homework question. At the bullet level:

- **Homework and Vignette only, no Exercise.** Next best alternative (both A1.1 bullets), AA-in-both-but-one-CA-each, and accept-or-reject trade each got their second rep from a vignette question written for the purpose; the Exercises still don't ask them.
- **Vignette only.** Slope as the OC of the horizontal-axis good is asked in Vignette A1 Q1 and nowhere else.
- **Exercise A1 Q3 has no pivot.** "Everyone's baking improves by a factor of 2" hits both goods, so it is two shifts. A fruitcake-only improvement would match the bullet.
- **Reciprocals** are computed in several places but never named as a question. Probably fine.
- **Specialization** (an A3.1 bullet) is practiced in Exercise A2, not Exercise A3, which differs from the Skillsheet's practice column but not from what students do.

## Next time

Aim for each bullet to appear in the Exercise and the Homework, with the Vignette as the third rep, so that the "2 of 3 unlocks" rule holds bullet by bullet rather than skill by skill.
