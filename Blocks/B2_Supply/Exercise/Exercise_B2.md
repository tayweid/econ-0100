Pumpkin pasties are produced by many sellers according to the following supply curve:

$$
P = 2 + \frac{1}{10} Q_s
$$

Prices are in galleons and quantity is in pasties.

### Q1 | Quantity Supplied

Find and plot the quantity supplied at a price of 10 galleons. What is the marginal cost at a quantity of 9 pasties?

### Q2 | Producer Surplus

Plot and find the area of producer surplus at a price of 10 galleons.
