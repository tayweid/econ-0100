# ECON 0100 | Exercise D3 | Public Goods

## Q1 | The Dumbledore Memorial

Three Dumbledores were considering whether to install a memorial of their brother Albus just outside their town. The individual marginal benefits for three ~~Dumbledors~~{++Dumbledores++} is below.

::: {.table style=grid columns="auto auto auto"}

| **Aurelius** | **Aberforth** | **Ariana** |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 75 | 10 | 20 |

:::

Since the statue will be installed in public space, any of the three are able to enjoy the statue regardless of whether they helped pay for it. It costs 90 galleons per year to operate and maintain the memorial.

### a) Visit Count

How many of the three Dumbledores would visit the memorial if it were built?

 

 

### b) Continued Funding

There's an endowment to fund the memorial for ~~it's~~{++its++} first year. Simply considering their financial interests, how many Dumbledores will contribute to the memorial's continued operation after the endowment runs out?

 

 

### c) The Referendum

Aurelius proposes a referendum that would raise residents’ taxes by 30 galleons per year, and puts it to a vote. If each ~~Dumbledor~~{++Dumbledore++} voted in their own private interests, would this referendum receive a majority vote?

 

 

### d) Marginal Social Benefit

What is the marginal social benefit from the memorial?

 

 

### e) Efficiency

If the referendum passed, would it lead to an efficient outcome?
