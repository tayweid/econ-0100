# Part D Practice Bank

One copy of every Part D practice handout from past semesters. Started 2026-10-13 with one file moved out of the Part C bank, and filled on 2026-10-21 from the three per-block `Practice_Bank` folders (D1 Games, D2 The Commons, D3 Public Goods and Voting), which are now gone. Names are `<Kind>_<Block>_<story>_<semester>`; the semester is the source's. PDFs and solutions sit beside their handout.

The old banks held many handouts twice or three times: whole extracts plus pieces split out per block, and reissues across semesters. One whole copy of each is kept here. Pieces, reissues and blank re-renders are in `_archive/_duplicates/`, named by their old block path (`D1_Games__Homework_D_v5.md` was `D1_Games/Practice_Bank/Homework_D_v5.md`). Only four byte-identical copies were deleted (two blank Classwork renders, the JA Vignette D1 solutions, a Katie Vignette D2 PDF); a copy of each is still here.
