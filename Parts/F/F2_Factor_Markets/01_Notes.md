# Episode F3: Labor Market

## Overview
This episode explores how labor markets work, including labor supply and demand, wage determination, and the factors that affect employment.

## Part 1: Labor Supply

### The Labor-Leisure Trade-off
- Time allocation between work and leisure
- Income effect vs substitution effect
- Backward-bending labor supply curve

### Factors Affecting Labor Supply
- Wage rates
- Non-wage benefits
- Working conditions
- Alternative income sources

## Part 2: Labor Demand

### Derived Demand for Labor
- Labor as an input to production
- Marginal product of labor
- Value of marginal product

### Factors Affecting Labor Demand
- Product demand
- Technology and productivity
- Prices of other inputs
- Market structure

## Part 3: Equilibrium in Labor Markets

### Wage Determination
- Competitive labor markets
- Supply and demand intersection
- Market clearing wages

### Labor Market Dynamics
- Adjustments to shocks
- Short-run vs long-run effects
- Geographic and occupational mobility

## Part 4: Labor Market Imperfections

### Market Power
- Monopsony (preview of F4)
- Labor unions
- Bilateral monopoly

### Government Intervention
- Minimum wage laws
- Employment protection
- Payroll taxes

## Key Takeaways
1. Labor markets connect to consumer choice through income generation
2. Wages are determined by supply and demand for labor
3. Labor markets have unique features that distinguish them from goods markets
4. Various imperfections and interventions affect labor market outcomes
