#### Molly's Problem

Molly Weasley recently started an hourly wage job as a Herbologist at a large Herbology firm and divides her income between Yorkshire puddings and roast beef sandwiches *(both of which are normal goods)*. In each of the consumer's problems below, show Yorkshire puddings on the horizontal axis and roast beef sandwiches on the vertical axis.

##### Q1 | New Yorkshire Pudding Stands
