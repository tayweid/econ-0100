#### The Hogsmeade Candy Shop Saga

#### Q1 | Advantages

Honeydukes and Zonkos are two candy shops in Hogsmeade, the only all-wizarding village in Britain. Both Honeydukes and Zonkos are operated by their owners and make two types of popular candy, Chocolate Frogs ($F$) and Ice Mice ($M$). Honeydukes, devoting all their resources to either good, can make $100$ pounds of $F$ or $75$ pounds of $M$. Zonkos can make $200$ pounds of $F$ or $100$ pounds of $M$. Which shop has the absolute advantage (AA) in Ice Mice, $M$? Which shop has the comparative advantage (CA) in Ice Mice, $M$? Two tables may be helpful in answering this question.
